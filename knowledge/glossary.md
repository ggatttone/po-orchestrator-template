## Agile & Delivery

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| DoR | Definition of Ready — 5 criteria a story must meet before sprint |
| DoD | Definition of Done — criteria for completing a story |
| Sprint | 2-week delivery cycle |
| Epic | Large body of work that spans multiple sprints |
| User Story | A unit of work written from the user's perspective |
| Spike | Investigation or research task (not a feature) |
| AC | Acceptance Criteria — testable conditions for story completion |

## Supply Chain & Operations

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Purchase Order (PO) | Document sent to supplier to order goods or services |
| Sales Order (SO) | Document from customer requesting goods or services |
| Lot / Batch | A group of items produced or received together, tracked for traceability |
| RDC | Regional Distribution Centre |
| SIPOC | Supplier, Input, Process, Output, Customer — process mapping framework |
| SCOR | Supply Chain Operations Reference model (ASCM) |
| GS1 | Global standards organization for supply chain barcodes and data |
